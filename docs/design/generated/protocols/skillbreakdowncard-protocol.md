# Protocol: SkillBreakdownCard (Kerala Rage)

## Design Identity: Solidarity Mode
- **Status**: New Construction
- **Primary Register**: Revelation & Technical Clarity
- **Core Narrative**: Inspecting the hidden structures of labor, verifying skills, revealing the blueprint of capability.

## Visual Hierarchy
- **Dominant**: Radial Score Gauge or Halo Disk (Z-2) highlighting the primary metric.
- **Primary Ink**: Ink Gold (#DAF674) for scores and optimism.
- **Secondary Ink**: Ripple Metal Blue (#48B3DA) for technical/metadata accents.
- **Substrate**: Charcoal Background with Blueprint Grid (8% opacity).

## Motion Physics
- **Entrance**: Scale Spring (600ms) with a focus on the Score Gauge "Inflating".
- **Interaction**: Sections expand/collapse with a physics-based "Stretch" (drag momentum).

## Anti-Slop Protocol
- **Banned**: border-radius 8px, Standard progress bars.
- **Required**: `radius-stone` for the breakdown cards.
- **Required**: `JetBrains Mono` for all numeric data and technical labels.
