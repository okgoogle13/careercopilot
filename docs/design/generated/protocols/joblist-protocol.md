# Protocol: JobList (Kerala Rage)

## Design Identity: Solidarity Mode
- **Status**: New Construction
- **Primary Register**: Discovery & Opportunity
- **Core Narrative**: Browsing collective openings, tactical placement, hybrid identity navigation.

## Visual Hierarchy
- **Dominant**: List of asymmetric `radius-stone` cards.
- **Primary Ink**: Smoke Green (#48DA8B) for calming yet active navigation.
- **Secondary Ink**: Signal Green (#48F0E5) for hybrid identity life in job tags.
- **Substrate**: Charcoal Background (#1A1A1A) with 22% grit opacity.

## Motion Physics
- **Entrance**: Staggered Stencil Slam (each item slams with 50ms delay).
- **Interaction**: Card lifts 4px on hover with shadow deepening.

## Anti-Slop Protocol
- **Banned**: border-radius 8px, Corporate blue.
- **Required**: `radius-stone` for job cards.
- **Required**: `JetBrains Mono` for technical metadata (salary, duration).
