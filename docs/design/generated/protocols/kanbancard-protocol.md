# Protocol: KanbanCard (Kerala Rage)

## Design Identity: Solidarity Mode
- **Status**: New Construction
- **Primary Register**: Control & Momentum
- **Core Narrative**: Organizing the steps of the cultivation cycle, maintaining tactical control over one's trajectory.

## Visual Hierarchy
- **Dominant**: `radius-stone` card with high-contrast sharp elevation shadow.
- **Primary Ink**: Solidarity Red (#F14714) for urgent tasks/deadlines.
- **Secondary Ink**: Smoke Green (#48DA8B) for stable/completed items.
- **Substrate**: Charcoal Background (#1A1A1A).

## Motion Physics
- **Interaction**: Drag Momentum (800ms) - feels "heavy" and tactile when moving.
- **Impact**: Stamp Impact on drop - spring elastic bounce.

## Anti-Slop Protocol
- **Banned**: border-radius 8px, Corporate kanban blue.
- **Required**: `radius-stone` for the cards.
- **Required**: `JetBrains Mono` for IDs and technical timestamps.
