# Kerala Rage — Design System Canon

> Canonical source of truth for visual identity, symbolic assets,
> AI generation rules, and cultural constraints.
> Changes require review.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: The Manifesto Press

**Kerala Rage** is not a SaaS product. It is a "Manifesto Press"—a tool for documenting professional history with the urgency of a street poster and the precision of an architectural archive.

- **We reject**: Light mode, bureaucratic forms, "delight," corporate Memphis, colonial aesthetics (Victorian/botanical).
- **We embrace**: Charcoal substrates, screenprint textures, "god rays" (linear gradients), monospaced data, resistance narratives.

---

## 2. Visual Identity Pillars

### A. The Substrate (Z-0)
Every screen begins with **Charcoal** (`#1A1A1A`). It is never flat choice. It is a physical material:
- **Paper**: Textured, fibrous, matte.
- **Concrete**: Urban, weathered, permanent.
- **Ink**: Screenprint aesthetic—multiply blends, overprinting, bleed.

### B. The Light (Z-1 to Z-3)
Light in the system is not "illumination"—it is **Revelation**.
- **Baru Gold** (`#DAF674`): The primary accent. Optimism, divinity, future.
- **Waratah Red** (`#F14714`): The alert. Resistance, urgent action, "Stop/Go."
- **Parrot Green** (`#48F0E5`): The vibration. Life, energy, "New."

### C. The Typography
Typography is structural, not decorative.
- **Display**: `Recursive Variable` (Range: Linear to Casual). Used for "Manifesto" headlines. Big, loud, ink-trapped.
- **Data**: `JetBrains Mono`. Used for "Evidence." Metadata, timestamps, hash IDs.
- **Body**: `Inter Variable`. Used for "Narrative." Clean, readable, providing contrast to the loud headers.

## 3. Symbolic Anchors & Cultural Constraints

The system uses specific cultural motifs ("Symbolic Anchors") to ground the interface in its Kerala diaspora and Australian solidarity context. These are **not stickers**. They are sacred or significant narrative elements.

### A. The Canon of Anchors

| Asset | Meaning | Usage Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shiva Nataraja** | Cosmic cycles, destruction/creation | **Reflective only.** Never mix with protest slogans. Max 1 per screen. |
| **Tipu Sultan** | Anti-colonial resistance | **Defiant only.** Companion to "Inquilab Zindabad." Hero sections. |
| **Bhagat Singh** | Revolutionary sacrifice | **Defiant only.** Hero sections. Minimum 96px size. |
| **First Nations Placard** | "Always Was, Always Will Be" | **Solidarity only.** Must appear *in situ* (on a placard/poster), never as a floating icon. |
| **Kerala Elephant** | Cultural richness, festivals | **Atmospheric.** Background element. Low opacity. |

### B. The Anti-Slop Protocol (Prohibited Motifs)

visuals that are STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
1. **The Crown**: No colonial symbols. No Victoria. No monarchy.
2. **The Passport**: No bureaucratic borders. No stamps that look like government approval.
3. **The Lotus**: No generic "spiritual" floral motifs. (Specific Kerala botanicals like Wattle/Waratah are allowed if treated as *specimens*).
4. **The Saffron Flag**: No political religious symbols outside of specific historical resistance contexts.
5. **Decoration**: No "doodles" or "corporate Memphis" humans.

## 4. AI Generation Rules

When creating assets (images, text) via AI:

1. **Prompt Structure**:
   - Always specify: "Screenprint style," "Halftone texture," "Charcoal background," "High contrast."
   - Never specify: "Modern UI," "Clean," "Minimalist," "Corporate."

2. **Texture Mandate**:
   - All assets must have grain.
   - Vectors must have "bleed" or "rough edges."
   - Gradients must be "dithered" or "linear" (God Rays), never radial/smooth.

3. **Content Safety**:
   - AI must *never* generate explanatory captions for cultural symbols (e.g., "Shiva is the destroyer"). The symbol speaks for itself.
   - AI must *never* mix competing religious/political symbols in one composition (e.g., Cross + Om + Crescent mashup).

## 5. Governance

- **Changes**: Modifications to this Canon require full team review.
- **Conflicts**: If a new feature conflicts with the Canon, the Canon wins.
- **Ambiguity**: Choose the interpretation that is *more* restrictive less decorative.
