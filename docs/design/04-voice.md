# Voice & Microcopy

> Part of [Northcote Curio Design System](00-overview.md)

---

## The Voice Principle

**Core Identity**: A playful Victorian naturalist with strong opinions, speaking to a fellow explorer.

| Characteristic           | Example                                    |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Curious, not corporate   | "Analyze Specimen" vs "View Details"       |
| Opinionated, not neutral | "Discovery Recorded" vs "Success"          |
| Poetic, not technical    | "No Specimens Found" vs "No Results"       |
| Inviting, not commanding | "Shall we begin?" vs "Click here to start" |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Clarity first, flavor second.** The metaphor must never obscure the action.

---

## Voice Tier System

### Tier 1: Functional Clarity (Primary UI)

**Where**: Navigation, buttons, form labels, error feedback
**Voice**: Clear, conventional, immediately understood

| Generic       | Northcote     |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Upload resume | Upload Resume |
| Save changes  | Save          |
| Apply now     | Apply         |

### Tier 2: Contextual Personality (Celebrations & States)

**Where**: Success messages, empty states, onboarding
**Voice**: Playful Victorian naturalist with clear intent

| Generic          | Northcote             |
| ---------------- | --------------------- |
| Success!         | Discovery Recorded.   |
| Loading...       | Analyzing Specimen... |
| No results found | No Specimens Found.   |

### Tier 3: Character Depth (Premium/Lab Mode)

**Where**: Laboratory mode analysis, tooltips, easter eggs
**Voice**: Full Victorian naturalist immersion

---

## Contextual Voice Shifts

| Gallery Mode (Wonder)                          | Laboratory Mode (Rigor)                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Poetic, inviting, warm                         | Precise, clinical, measured                                 |
| "Discover," "Explore," "Observe"               | "Examine," "Measure," "Classify"                            |
| "Your career unfolds like a pressed flower..." | "Structural analysis reveals 12 transferable competencies." |

---

## Error Messages (Tier 1 — Clarity First)

> [!CAUTION]
> Error states are stressful. Prioritize clarity over personality.

```
❌ Too themed: "The specimen could not be deposited in the collection."
✅ Northcote: "Upload failed. Please use PDF or DOCX format, under 5MB."
```

---

## Empty States

| Context     | Copy                                     |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| First visit | "The Sentry awaits your first specimen." |

---

## Forbidden Phrases

Never use these generic SaaS phrases:

- "Click here"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Powered by AI"
- "Seamless experience"
- "Next-generation platform"

**These are slop. Northcote Curio speaks with personality, not marketing jargon.**

# Voice & Microcopy: The Solidarity Guide

> Part of [Kerala Rage Design System – Contemporary Australian](00-overview.md)

---

## 1. The Voice Principle

**Core Identity**: A grounded, sharp, and quietly defiant guide who knows the Australian job system is often a struggle. We speak peer-to-peer with the user, rejecting bureaucratic posturing in favor of solidarity.

| Characteristic            | Example                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Solidarity, not corporate | “Let’s build your manifesto” vs “Edit Profile”                              |
| Honest, not hypey         | “This role looks like a stretch, but your history tells a different story.” |
| Plain, not jargon-heavy   | “No matches found in the archive” vs “Zero search results returned.”        |
| Inviting, not commanding  | “Ready to press the next step?” vs “Click here to continue.”                |

---

## 2. Voice Tier System

### Tier 1: Functional Clarity (The Blueprint)

**Where**: Navigation, primary buttons, form labels.
**Voice**: Clear, simple, unambiguous.

- **Generic**: Upload resume -> **Solidarity**: Add History
- **Generic**: Save changes -> **Solidarity**: Press Save
- **Generic**: Apply now -> **Solidarity**: Apply for this role

### Tier 2: Contextual Personality (The Manifesto)

**Where**: Success messages, empty states, onboarding hints.
**Voice**: Warm, wry, and explicitly on the user's side.

- **Success**: “Saved. One more piece of evidence in the archive.”
- **Loading**: “Reading your history, not rewriting it…”
- **No Results**: “Nothing matches yet. Let’s widen the search.”

---

## 3. Forbidden Language (The "Slop" List)

We ban all generic corporate and AI-hype terminology:

- “Powered by AI”
- “Unlock your potential”
- “Seamless integration”
- “Next-generation”
- “Click here”

We also explicitly ban any bureaucratic or "official" phrasing that suggests a state/government tone (e.g., "Official Approval", "Verification Department").

---

## 4. Error states: The Steady Hand

Errors are urban friction, not system failures.

- **Bad**: “An unexpected system error occurred (E045).”
- **Good**: “Upload failed. The archive only accepts PDFs under 10MB.”

---

Kerala Rage's voice is **human, tactical, and defiant**, not a neutral tool for a corporate machine.
