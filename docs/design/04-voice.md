# Voice & Microcopy: The Solidarity Guide

> Part of [Kerala Rage Design System – Contemporary Australian](00-overview.md)

---

## 1. The Voice Principle

**Core Identity**: A grounded, sharp, and quietly defiant guide who knows the Australian job system is often a struggle. We speak peer-to-peer with the user, rejecting bureaucratic posturing in favor of solidarity.

| Characteristic            | Example                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Solidarity, not corporate | “Let’s build your manifesto” vs “Edit Profile”                              |
| Honest, not hypey         | “This role looks like a stretch, but your history tells a different story.” |
| Plain, not jargon-heavy   | “No matches found in the archive” vs “Zero search results returned.”        |
| Inviting, not commanding  | “Ready to press the next step?” vs “Click here to continue.”                |

---

## 2. Voice Tier System

### Tier 1: Functional Clarity (The Blueprint)

**Where**: Navigation, primary buttons, form labels.
**Voice**: Clear, simple, unambiguous.

- **Generic**: Upload resume -> **Solidarity**: Add History
- **Generic**: Save changes -> **Solidarity**: Press Save
- **Generic**: Apply now -> **Solidarity**: Apply for this role

### Tier 2: Contextual Personality (The Manifesto)

**Where**: Success messages, empty states, onboarding hints.
**Voice**: Warm, wry, and explicitly on the user's side.

- **Success**: “Saved. One more piece of evidence in the archive.”
- **Loading**: “Reading your history, not rewriting it…”
- **No Results**: “Nothing matches yet. Let’s widen the search.”

---

## 3. Forbidden Language (The "Slop" List)

We ban all generic corporate and AI-hype terminology:

- “Powered by AI”
- “Unlock your potential”
- “Seamless integration”
- “Next-generation”
- “Click here”

We also explicitly ban any bureaucratic or "official" phrasing that suggests a state/government tone (e.g., "Official Approval", "Verification Department").

---

## 4. Error states: The Steady Hand

Errors are urban friction, not system failures.

- **Bad**: “An unexpected system error occurred (E045).”
- **Good**: “Upload failed. The archive only accepts PDFs under 10MB.”

---

Kerala Rage's voice is **human, tactical, and defiant**, not a neutral tool for a corporate machine.
