## Page Specifications

### 1. Landing Page ("The Resurrection")

**Mode**: Gallery

- **Background**: Void with `nocturnal-canopy.png` (25%), bottom-up gradient
- **Hero**: "FUTURE SPECIMEN" in Libre Bodoni 96px Gold
- **Features**: 3 glassmorphic Stone cards (Discovery, Analysis, Application)
- **Nav**: Floating dock with Pebble buttons

### 2. Authentication ("The Entry Gate")

**Mode**: Gallery

- **Card**: 480px Stone container, high-blur glassmorphism
- **Inputs**: Lens style with Gold bottom border
- **Action**: Solid Gold Pebble button

### 3. Onboarding ("Choosing the Soil")

**Mode**: Gallery

- **Layout**: 3×3 grid of Leaf cards
- **Selected State**: Gold filament border + glow
- **Content**: Industry sectors with botanical icons

### 4. Ingestion ("The Mulch")

**Mode**: Laboratory

- **Background**: Cream with skeleton watermark (6%)
- **Drop Zone**: 900px sunken Stone container
- **Label**: "DEPOSIT SPECIMEN (PDF)" in JetBrains Mono

### 5. Analysis Dashboard ("The Audit Microscope")

**Mode**: Laboratory

- **Layout**: 40/60 split
- **Left**: Large brass compass gauge
- **Right**: 2×2 grid of Stone cards with skill metrics

### 6. Opportunity Feed ("The Sentry Lookout")

**Mode**: Gallery

- **Header**: Kookaburra perched on nav
- **Cards**: Glassmorphic Stone with Seed badges ("98% Match")

### 7. Kanban Board ("The Greenhouse")

**Mode**: Gallery

- **Layout**: Horizontal glass columns (trellises)
- **Cards**: Leaf-shaped tickets
- **Metrics**: Massive thin numbers ("12 ACTIVE")

### 8. Split-Screen Editor ("The Workbench")

**Mode**: Laboratory

- **Layout**: 50/50 vertical split
- **Left**: Matte editor area, Gold focus border
- **Right**: Specimen drawer with draggable Stone cards

### 9. Studio Designer ("Specimen Finalization")

**Mode**: Laboratory

- **Background**: Aged parchment grain
- **Toggle**: "Bot View" for anatomical wireframe preview

### 10. Settings ("The Archive Vault")

**Mode**: Laboratory

- **Layout**: Single centered column
- **Cards**: Solid Stone with brass gear icons
- **Typography**: JetBrains Mono labels

### 11. Dashboard ("The Canopy View")

**Mode**: Gallery

- **Layout**: Hero metric bar + 2×2 card grid
- **Decoration**: Ceiling gum asset hanging top-left
- **Interaction**: Blur bloom on hover
