## Batch 1: Devotional & Resistance (The Anchors)

### `kr-solidarity__devotional__shiva-statue__v1.png`
**Category:** Devotional / Hero | **Mode:** kr-dark

#### 1. Visual Definition
- **Concept:** The "Cosmic Dancer" in repose. Not a cartoon, but a photograph of a black stone statue screened onto the page.
- **Materiality:** "Granite & Gold." Heavy, ancient stone texture with a muted, flaking gold halo.
- **Lighting:** Monumental. Top-down "temple light" that casts deep shadows, hiding details in the charcoal void.
- **Framing:** Centered or asymmetric balance. monumental scale.

#### 2. Usage Rules
- **Placement:** Landing Page Hero or "Manifesto" intro.
- **Restriction:** NEVER mix with protest slogans. This is a moment of silence.
- **Technical Specs:** Aspect Ratio 1:1 (Square) | Min Resolution: 2048x2048px

#### 3. Optimized Prompt (Nano Banana Pro)
**Generation Prompt:**
> Screenprint illustration of a Shiva statue, stone-like and monumental. Heavy ancient granite texture with a muted, flaking gold halo. Top-down "temple light" casting deep shadows into a charcoal void. Centered, icon-scale composition. 3-5 ink colors, matte charcoal background, rough edges, slight misregistration. **No text.**
