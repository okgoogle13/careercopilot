## Complete Production Prompt

```
Create a Victorian naturalist botanical illustration plate in the style of 19th-century Australian scientific documentation.

=== VISUAL STYLE ===
- Style: Antique botanical plate from a natural history museum
- Era: Victorian-era scientific illustration (1850-1890)
- Medium: Detailed gouache and watercolor rendering on dark background
- Mood: Scholarly precision with artistic beauty

=== BACKGROUND ===
- Deep charcoal-black gallery wall background (#1A1A1A)
- Matte finish, museum exhibition presentation
- No gradients, no vignettes — flat dark field

=== SUBJECTS ===
Include 5-7 Australian native specimens arranged organically:

Primary focal specimen (largest, center-left):
- Telopea speciosissima (NSW Waratah) — coral-red flower with green leaves

Supporting specimens:
- Chlamydosaurus kingii (Frill-necked Lizard) — displaying frill in terracotta/ochre
- Acacia pycnantha (Golden Wattle) — clusters of golden-yellow pompom flowers
- Banksia serrata (Old Man Banksia) — seed cone with spiky brown texture
- Tachyglossus aculeatus (Echidna) — spine detail or full specimen
- Sparassidae (Huntsman Spider) — honey-brown translucent
- Eucalyptus leaves — sage green with curved form

Additional elements:
- Agate or geode cross-section slice
- Dried seed pods and small nuts scattered organically
- Small gastropod (snail shell) for scale reference

=== COLOR PALETTE ===
Strict Australian botanical palette only:
- Coral-red: Waratah flower (#C94A4A)
- Terracotta: Lizard frill, earth tones (#B8623B)
- Ochre/Gold: Wattle, accents (#D4A435)
- Sage green: Eucalyptus leaves (#7A8B6E)
- Warm brown: Banksia, spider, pods (#8B6B4E)
- Cream/Bone: Specimen labels (#D4C4A8)

DO NOT USE: Purple, blue, neon colors, or generic gradients

=== COMPOSITION ===
- Asymmetrical arrangement — NOT grid-aligned
- Organic spacing between specimens — as if arranged by a naturalist curator
- Visual hierarchy: Waratah as hero element, others supporting
- Depth layering: foreground details, midground specimens, background leaves
- Cabinet-of-curiosities aesthetic
- Slight overlap between elements is encouraged

=== TYPOGRAPHY & LABELING ===
Each specimen must have Victorian scientific labeling:

Format:
Fig. [Roman numeral]. [Scientific binomial name in italics]
([Common name])

Examples:
"Fig. I. Telopea speciosissima (Waratah)"
"Fig. II. Chlamydosaurus kingii (Frill-necked Lizard)"
"Fig. III. Acacia pycnantha (Golden Wattle)"

Label styling:
- Font: Elegant serif (similar to Lora or Crimson Text)
- Size: Clearly legible, approximately 3-4% of image height
- Color: Warm cream (#D4C4A8)
- Position: Bottom-right of each specimen
- Subtle text shadow for readability on dark background

=== ADDITIONAL DETAILS ===
- Include one small compass rose or decorative element in bottom-right corner
- Add plate attribution: "Northcote Curio Collection — Plate [Roman numeral]"
- Cross-section or cutaway details welcome (agate slice, seed pod interior)
- Fine stippling or hatching texture on specimens for authenticity
- No modern elements, frames, or device mockups

=== RENDERING QUALITY ===
- Hyperdetailed, museum-quality illustration
- Sharp focus on botanical accuracy
- Realistic textures: scales, petals, fur, chitin
- Dramatic lighting from above-left
- Professional scientific illustration standard

=== NEGATIVE PROMPT (What to avoid) ===
- No cartoon or simplified style
- No photorealistic photography
- No modern fonts (no sans-serif)
- No purple or blue color schemes
- No symmetrical grid layouts
- No white backgrounds
- No generic stock illustration style
- No AI-looking artifacts or uncanny elements
```
