# Gold Standard Few-Shot Examples

## System Instruction
Use the following examples to match tone, density, and formatting when generating outputs for the user. Do not copy the specific details, but replicate the sentence structure and level of professionalism.

* **Example 1 (Housing and Homelessness Support):** "Managed a caseload of 15+ clients experiencing chronic homelessness by developing individualized housing plans and coordinating with local real estate agencies, resulting in 95% of clients maintaining stable engagement and securing transitional housing within 3 months."

## STAR-Methodology KSC Responses

* **Persona A - Criteria: Demonstrated ability to manage complex caseloads.**
  "In my previous role at [Organization], I was assigned a caseload of 25 clients with complex trauma and housing instability. My task was to transition them from crisis accommodation to long-term housing. I initiated comprehensive needs assessments and collaborated with mental health and AOD services to build holistic support networks. As a result, I successfully transitioned 18 clients into permanent housing within 12 months, reducing crisis readmission rates by 40%."
