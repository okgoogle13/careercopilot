# kerala-rage kr-solidarity Design Philosophy

## Core Principles

### KR Streetprint + Migrant Rage Solidarity

kerala-rage is a design system rooted in migrant worker resistance, not Australian nature. It bridges:

- **Street-protest aesthetics**: Wheat-paste posters, stencil typography, resistance portraiture
- **Diaspora identity**: Kerala cultural anchors (devotional motifs, elephant, landscape) expressed through a Melbourne laneway lens
- **First Nations solidarity**: In situ only — on placards and posters, never as decorative system theming

### Typography as Meaning-Making Device

Typography is not decoration — it's declaration. The **Solidarity Stack** is the only authorized voice:

| Role | Font | Variable Axes |
|---|---|---|
| **Workhorse** | Work Sans | `wght` 100–900, `GRAD` for hover |
| **Expressive** | Fraunces | `SOFT`, `WONK`, `opsz`, `wght` |
| **Proclamation** | Libre Bodoni | High weight contrast for declarations |
| **Technical** | JetBrains Mono | Code, data, annotations |
| **Curator** | Caveat | Handwritten flourishes, signatures |
| **Color Accent** | Nabla (COLRv1) | Icon-scale hero moments only |

> Every font axis is a weapon: weight = labor pressure, width = collective breath, grade = interaction pulse.

### Color as Resistance

The palette is grounded in **streets and struggle**, not Australian ecology:

- **Step 0** `#0F0F0F` — canvas; never white
- **Solidarity Red** `#F14714` — primary CTA, screenprint heat
- **Ink Gold** `#DAF674` — temple radiance, halo disks, optimism
- **Stencil Yellow** `#F6E748` — attention markers, poster type
- **Charcoal ramps** — Melbourne asphalt, substrate depth

**Explicitly forbidden**: Flora, ochre-from-native-plants, wattle, sage, terracotta, Australian endemic palette.

### Layout as Collective Rhythm

- Asymmetric radii: **Stone / Slab / Pebble** shapes — never `border-radius: 50%`
- 8px grid with poster-like disruptions
- Wheat-paste torn edges for section breaks
- Industrial rivet dividers for navigation groups

## The Three Emotional States

### Solidarity Protest
Expanded, heavy typography. Collective breath. `wdth: 120`, `wght: 800`.

### Labor Pressure
Compressed, black weight. Exhaustion and constraint. `wdth: 75`, `wght: 900`.

### Melancholy Longing
Mid-weight oscillation. Between-worlds sections. `wght: 475`, `wdth: 98`, `SOFT: 50`.

## What kerala-rage IS

- ✅ Migrant rage made visible — diaspora identity without apology
- ✅ KR streetprint stencil aesthetic in digital UI
- ✅ Variable font axes expressing pressure, relief, and solidarity
- ✅ Palette of streets, laneways, and resistance portraiture
- ✅ First Nations solidarity only in situ — never appropriated as decoration

## What kerala-rage IS NOT

- ❌ Australian endemic flora or nature motifs (NO BOTANICAL ELEMENTS)
- ❌ Generic corporate design (Inter, Roboto, blue gradients)
- ❌ Light mode or white backgrounds
- ❌ Perfect circles (`border-radius: 50%`)
- ❌ Crown/monarchy or bureaucratic aesthetics
- ❌ Overuse of slogans (slogan wallpaper)
