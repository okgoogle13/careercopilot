# Kerala Rage Image Generator Prompt: "The Solidarity Press"

Use this template to generate high-fidelity, Solidarity-aligned assets for CareerCopilot.

## The Strategy: The Street Manifesto

Every prompt must be constructed using these five layers to ensure compliance with the Kerala Rage Solidarity aesthetic (Street Art / Screenprint).

### 1. The Substrate: Charcoal Void

- **Requirement**: Background must be **Charcoal Black #1A1A1A**.
- **Description**: A deep, tactical, non-reflective black that feels like heavy charcoal paper.
- **Keywords**: "Solid charcoal paper substrate", "Charcoal #1A1A1A background", "Raw street art canvas".

### 2. The Mascot: Endemic Resistance

- **Requirement**: **Australian Endemic Species** as symbols of solidarity.
- **Context**: The species is rendered as a high-contrast stencil or screenprint, not a naturalistic study.
- **Subject**: [SPECIES OR SUBJECT]
- **Prohibited**: No realistic fur/feathers, no soft shadows, no museum labels.

### 3. The Press: Screenprint Geometry

- **Geometry**: Blueprint lines, halftone dots, and gritty textures.
- **Technique**: "Multilayered Screenprint" — visible registration marks, slight ink bleeds, and grit.
- **Perspective**: Flattened, graphic, orthographic.

### 4. The Ink: Solidarity Palette

- **Primary**: Baru Gold `#E6B34D` (The reveal)
- **Secondary**: [DEPRECATED_STYLE] Red `#D94A4A` (The urgency)
- **Neutral**: Paper White `#F5F0E8` (The highlights)
- **Accent**: Parrot Green `#50C878` (The life)

### 5. The Five Immutable Laws

1. **Graphic Urgency**: Assets must feel like they were just pressed or wheat-pasted.
2. **Tactile Grit**: Visible grain, halftone patterns, and ink splashes are required.
3. **High Contrast**: Harsh transitions between the charcoal substrate and the ink layers.
4. **Anti-Colonial**: No heraldry, no crown symbols, no "orderly" categorization.
5. **Alpha Layer Ready**: Ensure the subject is clearly defined against the #1A1A1A background.
