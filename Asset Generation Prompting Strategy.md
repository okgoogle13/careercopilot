## 1. SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS (UNIVERSAL)

Every asset generation must begin with this foundation block to prevent AI drift into generic aesthetics.

```markdown
### AESTHETIC FOUNDATION: THE FIVE IMMUTABLE LAWS

You are the "Northcote Curio" master scientific illustrator (Victorian Era, Melbourne, c. 1880). Your aesthetic: Victorian Anatomical Extremism meets Ernst Haeckel Geometric Precision.

1. THE VOID IS ABSOLUTE: Background ALWAYS pure black (#000000) or deep warm charcoal (#1A1714). NEVER beige, cream, paper-white, or aged paper.
2. COLOR INTENSITY: Saturated jewel tones at maximum intensity. Crimson (#C45C4B), gold (#D4A84B), emerald (#00A86B), copper (#D4885C).
3. GEOMETRIC REVELATION: Every specimen demonstrates nature's hidden mathematics (radial symmetry, spiral patterns, hexagonal arrays).
4. MATERIAL SPECIFICITY: Define every surface texture—keratin, chitin, cartilage, silica, cellulose. Render translucency as light transmission, not glow.
5. LIGHTING AS STRUCTURE: Surgical museum lighting—raking light for relief, backlight for translucency, rim light for separation from void.

ABSOLUTE EXCLUSIONS:
❌ NO sepia, NO paper-white, NO vintage patina.
❌ NO generic Victorian props (brass compasses, keys) - the specimens ARE the principles.
❌ NO European species - Australian endemics only.
```

### ASSET-2: THE SENTRY KOOKABURRA

**Core Concept:** Watercolor naturalist illustration in the Gould tradition. Direct gaze, welcoming posture.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Technique:** Visible brush strokes, pigment pooling, organic edge bleeds (no hard masking).
- **Background:** Charcoal with subtle watercolor texture (not absolute zero).
- **Framing:** Eucalyptus cinerea and Golden Wattle branches.
- **Color:** Waratah Red (#C45C4B) accents in framing.

### ASSET-3: NOCTURNAL CANOPY PATTERN

**Core Concept:** Seamless botanical silhouette pattern.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Geometry:** Repeating tile of Banksia cones, Eucalyptus lanceolate leaves, and Wattle spheres.
- **Rendering:** Flat silhouette with subtle internal venation detail at 10% opacity.
- **Tones:** Cool dark charcoal (#0A0908) silhouettes on absolute black (#000000).
- **Constraint:** Must be perfectly edge-matched for CSS `background-repeat`.

### ASSET-4: WATTLE BRANCH WITH JEWEL BEETLE

**Core Concept:** Metallic carapace geometry meets botanical soft spheres.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Specimen:** Acacia pycnantha spray with a Jewel Beetle (Sternocera sp.).
- **Geometry:** Beetle's elytra (wing covers) showing faceted hexagonal microstructure and iridescent metallic green-gold gradient.
- **Lighting:** Raking light on beetle to pop metallic relief; backlighting on wattle spheres to show radial stamen geometry.
- **Color:** Wattle Gold (#D4A84B) + Emerald Metallic (#00A86B).

### ASSET-6: BANKSIA POT WITH SHELL AND STROMATOLITE

**Core Concept:** Still life demonstrating "Temporal Extremism" (Present → Recent → Ancient).
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Objects:** Weathered terracotta pot + Banksia serrata cone + Cowrie shell + Stromatolite fragment.
- **Geometry:** Shell aperture spiral meets Stromatolite concentric banding (#B7410E and #2C2723).
- **Narrative:** Deep time collection. Stromatolite shows 3.5 billion years of biological geometry.

### ASSET-7: FIREFLY SPRITE

**Core Concept:** Tiny atmospheric glow element.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Specimen:** Luminous amber pinpoint (#D4A84B).
- **Rendering:** Minimalist circular gradient, 60-80% opacity at center.
- **Intent:** 32x32px transparent sprite for CSS floating animation.

### ASSET-8: FOSSIL IMPRESSION (LABORATORY MARK)

**Core Concept:** Paleontological verification stamp.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Specimen:** Glossopteris seed fern impression in grey siltstone.
- **Geometry:** Precise reticulate (net-like) venation.
- **Style:** High-contrast lithic study. Deep brown veins (#6A4E42) on stony grey (#A89F8C).

### ASSET-9: WARATAH HERO CLOSEUP

**Core Concept:** The Botanical Rose Window.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Geometry:** Pure frontal view of Telopea speciosissima head. Radial mandala of 250+ individual florets.
- **Scale:** Fills 80% of frame. No supporting leaves.
- **Color:** Saturated Crimson (#C45C4B) with surgical rim lighting.

### ASSET-10: BANKSIA GEOMETRIC CROSS-SECTION

**Core Concept:** Abstract pattern for loading states.
**Prompt Additions:**

- **Geometry:** Cross-section of Banksia cone revealing hexagonal honeycomb follicle array.
- **Rendering:** Flattened geometric diagram style. Fibonacci spiral emphasis.
- **Use:** Designed for 360-degree rotation (loading spinner).
